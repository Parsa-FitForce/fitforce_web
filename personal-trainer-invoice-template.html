What Trainers Should Include

Cleaner invoices reduce awkward payment follow-up and make tax season easier.

Clear line items

Separate single sessions, assessments, packages, and recurring training. Clients should understand what they are paying for without texting for clarification.

Payment terms

State whether payment is due on receipt, before the first session, or on a recurring date. Include accepted payment methods and late-payment expectations.

Package details

For session packs, include the number of sessions, expiration policy, cancellation rules, and whether unused sessions roll over.

Tax records

Keep invoices connected to client payments and business expenses. A consistent invoice format makes bookkeeping much less painful later.
