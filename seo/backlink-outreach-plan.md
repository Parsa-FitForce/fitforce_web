# FitForce Backlink Outreach Plan

## Campaign Goal

Earn relevant, non-spammy backlinks to FitForce by pitching useful AI and trainer-business resources, especially:

- `/personal-trainer-ai-readiness-checklist`
- `/personal-trainer-client-intake-form-template`
- `/personal-trainer-invoice-template`
- `/blog/what-is-an-mcp-server-for-personal-trainers`
- `/blog/ai-agents-personal-training-business`

The backlink angle is not "please link to our SaaS." The angle is: FitForce published a practical resource that helps personal trainers understand and prepare for AI-assisted business operations.

## Tracking Rules

Track each prospect with:

- Domain
- Page or publication
- Contact person
- Contact URL/email
- Segment
- Asset pitched
- Date contacted
- Follow-up date
- Status
- Link URL if won
- Notes

Follow-up cadence:

- Day 0: first email
- Day 5: short follow-up
- Day 14: final follow-up only if the fit is strong

Avoid:

- Paid link placements
- Link exchanges
- Low-quality directories
- Mass guest-post farms
- Exact-match anchor demands
- Asking for homepage links when the resource is a better fit

Preferred anchors:

- "AI readiness checklist for personal trainers"
- "personal trainer client intake form template"
- "personal trainer invoice template"
- "AI workflows for personal trainers"
- "MCP server for personal trainers"
- "personal trainer business automation"
- Natural branded mentions like "FitForce"
