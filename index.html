Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from personal trainers about running their business and how FitForce helps.

Most independent trainers end up using separate tools for scheduling, payments, waivers, and accounting — often five or six different apps. FitForce combines client management, online booking, payment processing, digital waivers, expense tracking, and certification management into a single platform built specifically for fitness professionals.

Yes — professional liability insurance is essential for personal trainers. It protects you if a client is injured during a session or claims your advice caused harm. Most gyms and studios require it, and it's critical for independent trainers working on their own. FitForce includes liability coverage options and digital waivers so you're protected from day one.

One of the biggest challenges for self-employed trainers is finding affordable health insurance without an employer plan. FitForce gives you access to group health plans through our network, so you can get better rates than shopping the individual market alone. It's one of the most requested features from our early members.

As a self-employed trainer, you need to track all business income, deductible expenses (equipment, continuing education, mileage, gym fees), and estimated quarterly tax payments. FitForce lets you log income and expenses as you go and export clean reports at tax time — no more digging through bank statements or scrambling in April.

Personal trainers typically need to maintain a primary certification (NASM, ACE, ACSM, NSCA, or ISSA), CPR/AED certification, and any specialty certifications for their niche. Most require continuing education credits for renewal every one to two years. FitForce stores all your credentials in one place and sends alerts before they expire so you never lapse.
